Questions

Before you buy.

What is a Desk Bot?

A ready-made Grok Bot template for one front-desk job. You buy it once on Gumroad, import it into Grok Bot, and connect the tools you already use.

Will it message anyone without me?

No. They draft. You approve. Nothing goes out without your OK.

What do I need to run one?

Grok Bot. Missed Call Desk and Confirm Desk also need your own SMS number, Quo or Twilio-class. We don't supply phone numbers.

Does Payment Chase Desk take the payment?

No. It tracks unpaid invoices and drafts reminders that get firmer over time. It stops when they pay or reply. The desks don't take payments or deposits.

Will it post reviews or social posts for me?

No. Review Desk never auto-posts; you copy the replies you approve. Social Post Desk never posts for you. Neither writes fake reviews.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Each desk is a one-time purchase. Gumroad delivers the setup guide instantly, with the import link inside.
