BasketWise

Enter your shopping list

Type it, speak it, or photograph a written list. Then we’ll compare shops and plan your trip.

Say items with “and” or commas, or snap a clear photo of a written list.

Which stores do you want to use?

Pick the supermarkets to include. You can shop at the single cheapest, or split the list across shops for the lowest item prices.

How should we plan your trip?

Used later for turn-by-turn maps. For now we build a simple visit order.

Price comparison

Shelf totals for your list across the stores you picked.

Your shopping journey

Based on the prices — either one stop at the cheapest total, or a split journey with how much of the basket each store covers.

Lock-screen store lists

Inspired by apps like There — pin each shop’s item list as a phone notification so it can show on your lock screen while you shop.

On iPhone: Add BasketWise to your Home Screen, then allow notifications for best results.

BasketWise uses illustrative UK supermarket prices unless a live Hobnight key is configured. Always check live prices before you buy.
